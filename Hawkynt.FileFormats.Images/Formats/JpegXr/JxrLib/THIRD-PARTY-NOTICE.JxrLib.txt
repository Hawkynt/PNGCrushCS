The JPEG XR decoder in this folder (namespace FileFormat.JpegXr.JxrLib) is converted from Microsoft's
jxrlib, as maintained at https://github.com/4creators/jxrlib:

  image/sys/strcodec.c, strcodec.h       bit I/O, tile and quantiser bookkeeping, index tables
  image/sys/adapthuff.c                  adaptive VLC tables and discriminant adaptation
  image/sys/strPredQuant.c, image.c      quantiser mapping, prediction modes, scan tables, models
  image/sys/strTransform.c               2x2 transform kernels
  image/sys/windowsmediaphoto.h, common.h  enumerations and limits
  image/decode/strdec.c                  headers, decode driver, output stage (outputMBRow)
  image/decode/segdec.c, decode.c        entropy decoding and coding contexts
  image/decode/strInvTransform.c         inverse transform and overlap filters
  image/decode/strPredQuantDec.c         inverse prediction and dequantisation
  jxrgluelib/JXRGlueJxr.c, JXRGlue.c     container parsing, pixel format table, PKImageDecode_Copy_WMP
