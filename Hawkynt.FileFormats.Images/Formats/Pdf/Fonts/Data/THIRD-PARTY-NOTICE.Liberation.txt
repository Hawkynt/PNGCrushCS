Liberation fonts 2.1.5
======================

Substitutes/*.ttf.br are the twelve TrueType files of Liberation Sans, Liberation
Serif and Liberation Mono, version 2.1.5 (https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts),
each compressed with Brotli. Decompressing one gives the original file byte
for byte; the fonts are not subset, renamed or otherwise modified, so they are
the Font Software as the copyright holders distribute it and keep their
Reserved Font Names.

They are used to draw text in PDF documents that name one of the standard
Helvetica, Times or Courier faces without embedding it. They are bundled with,
not sold as, this software, and remain under the SIL Open Font License 1.1,
whose full text is in Substitutes/OFL-Liberation.txt (also embedded in the
assembly as FileFormat.Pdf.Fonts.Data.Substitutes.OFL-Liberation.txt).
