3. Adobe Core 14 AFM files
--------------------------
The widths in StandardFontMetrics.cs are the WX values of the Core 14 AFM
files (Helvetica.afm and the rest), reached through pdf.js's metrics.js and
checked against the AFM files. Modification note: only the advance widths are
carried, re-expressed as a table keyed by glyph name; nothing else of the AFM
files is included. Each AFM file carries a copyright line of the form

  Copyright (c) 1985, 1987, 1989, 1990, 1997 Adobe Systems Incorporated.
  All Rights Reserved.

(that one is Helvetica.afm's; the years differ from file to file), and the
ReadMe that accompanies them reads:

  This file and the 14 PostScript(R) AFM files it accompanies may be used,
  copied, and distributed for any purpose and without charge, with or without
  modification, provided that all copyright notices are retained; that the AFM
  files are not distributed without this file; that all modifications to this
  file or any of the AFM files are prominently noted in the modified file(s);
  and that this paragraph is not modified. Adobe Systems has no responsibility
  or obligation to support the use of the AFM files.

5. Adobe cmap-resources (BSD 3-Clause)
--------------------------------------
CMaps/cmaps.bin.br carries the CMap files of
https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources (every current character
collection and Adobe-Manga1; not the deprecated Adobe-Japan2 set, the UTF-8
encodings or the CID-to-CID Adobe-<ordering>-<supplement> maps), repacked by
CMaps/pack_cmaps.py into a compact binary form with every codespace range,
CID range, CID mapping, notdef range, usecmap and WMode kept, and compressed
with Brotli. Copyright 1990-2023 Adobe. The licence text travels beside it as
CMaps/LICENSE-cmap-resources.txt and inside the assembly.


5a. Adobe mapping-resources-pdf (BSD 3-Clause)
----------------------------------------------
CMaps/cid2unicode.bin.br carries the four ToUnicode CMaps of
https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/mapping-resources-pdf (pdf2unicode/
Adobe-CNS1-UCS2, Adobe-GB1-UCS2, Adobe-Japan1-UCS2, Adobe-Korea1-UCS2), every
CID they map to a single character, repacked by CMaps/pack_cid_unicode.py as
delta-coded runs and compressed with Brotli. Copyright 1990-2019 Adobe. The
licence text travels beside it as CMaps/LICENSE-mapping-resources-pdf.txt and
inside the assembly.


6. Noto fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
----------------------------------------
Substitutes/NotoSymbolSubstitute.ttf.br is a subset of Noto Sans Math 3.000,
Noto Sans Symbols 2 2.008, Noto Sans Symbols 2.003 and Noto Sans 2.015
(https://github.com/notofonts), the characters of the Symbol and ZapfDingbats
encodings recombined into one renamed font by
Substitutes/make_symbol_substitute.py. Copyright 2022 Google LLC and 2022 The
Noto Project Authors. See Substitutes/OFL-Noto.txt, which also travels inside
the assembly.
