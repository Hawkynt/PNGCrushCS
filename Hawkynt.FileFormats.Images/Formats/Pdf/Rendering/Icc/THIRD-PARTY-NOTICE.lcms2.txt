Parts of the code in this folder are converted from Little CMS 2
(https://github.com/mm2/Little-CMS), Copyright (c) 1998-2026 Marti Maria Saguer,
licensed under the MIT License.

Converted from Little CMS (src/):

  cmstypes.c   curv, para, XYZ, lut8 (mft1), lut16 (mft2), lutAtoB (mAB) and
               lutBtoA (mBA) tag readers                      -> IccProfile.cs
  cmsio1.c     _cmsReadInputLUT, _cmsReadOutputLUT, the grey and RGB
               matrix-shaper pipelines, cmsIsMatrixShaper,
               cmsIsIntentSupported, _cmsReadMediaWhitePoint   -> IccProfile.cs
  cmsintrp.c   LinLerp1D, Eval1Input, BilinearInterp16, TrilinearInterp16,
               TetrahedralInterp16, Eval4Inputs..Eval15Inputs  -> IccPipeline.cs
  cmsgamma.c   DefaultEvalParametricFn, cmsEvalToneCurveFloat  -> IccPipeline.cs
  cmslut.c     the curve, matrix, CLUT and Lab/XYZ stages      -> IccPipeline.cs
  cmspcs.c     cmsLab2XYZ, cmsXYZ2Lab                          -> IccPipeline.cs
  cmswtpnt.c   _cmsAdaptationMatrix (Bradford),
               _cmsBuildRGB2XYZtransferMatrix                  -> IccPipeline.cs,
                                                                  IccTransform.cs
  cmscnvrt.c   DefaultICCintents, ComputeConversion, AddConversion,
               ComputeBlackPointCompensation, IsEmptyLayer     -> IccTransform.cs
  cmssamp.c    cmsDetectBlackPoint, BlackPointAsDarkerColorant,
               BlackPointUsingPerceptualBlack                  -> IccTransform.cs
  cmsvirt.c    cmsCreate_sRGBProfile                           -> IccTransform.cs

Constants copied verbatim: the D50 white, the Bradford cone matrix, the
perceptual black, the 1.15 XYZ encoding limit and the sRGB curve parameters.
