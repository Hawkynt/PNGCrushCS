Parts of the H.265 format range extensions support in this folder were converted from the JCT-VC
HEVC reference software (HM), release HM-16.20, https://vcgit.hhi.fraunhofer.de/jvet/HM :

  - the limited exponential-Golomb escape binarization of extended precision processing
    (H265Residual.DecodeRemaining, from TDecSbac::xReadCoefRemainExGolomb);
  - the combined shift of transform-skipped residuals and its clamp under extended precision
    (H265Transform.Skip, from TComTrQuant::xITransformSkip);
  - the rotation of losslessly coded intra 4x4 residuals and the gating of the intra boundary
    filters by implicit residual DPCM (from TComTrQuant::invTransformNxN and
    TComPrediction::predIntraAng).

The constants of the new CABAC contexts are those of ITU-T H.265 Tables 9-5 to 9-37.

Parts of the H.265 screen content coding support in this folder follow the HM screen content
model, release HM-16.21+SCM-8.8, from the same repository, where its behaviour settles what the
normative text leaves to be read:

  - the size condition of the 8x8 bi-prediction restriction beside a current picture reference,
    taken as the prediction block's own width and height of at most eight
    (H265MotionPrediction.RestrictSmallBiprediction, from TComDataCU::isBipredRestriction and
    TComDataCU::is8x8BipredRestriction);
  - where a transposed palette coded unit in a subsampled chroma format reads its index map and
    its escape values (H265FrameDecoder._ReconstructPalette and _DecodePaletteEscapes, from
    TDecCu::xDecodePaletteTexture and TDecSbac::parsePaletteModeSyntax).

The constants of the screen content CABAC contexts are those of ITU-T H.265 Tables 9-38 to 9-42.
