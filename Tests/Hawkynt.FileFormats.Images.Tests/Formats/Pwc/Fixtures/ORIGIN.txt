PWC fixtures and what produced them
===================================

Every picture here was generated for this repository (rectangles, scattered pixels, a gradient, a flat
fill) and written as a BMP. Nothing in this directory comes from the PWC distribution itself.

  NAME__OPTION.pwc   written by pwcencode.exe 1.5.1 from that BMP with the switch named by OPTION:
                       default   (no switch)
                       noq2      -noq2
                       noq5      -noq5
                       gray      -gray
                       flip      -flip
                       lps3      -maxlps 3
                       lps30     -maxlps 30
  NAME__OPTION.bmp   pwcdecode.exe 1.5.1's decode of the .pwc beside it - the captured oracle output
                     the reader is compared against, and the picture the writer is asked to re-encode
                     to the very same bytes.

Both programs were run under Wine 11.22 (WINEDEBUG=-all), from pwc.zip, the author's 1999 demonstration
package (sha256 b7a56255e413e81257fca6d309621cc16636013c91cc83372c2547443dea6662):

  pwcencode.exe  sha256 703fe67f6b5c53a635d0bec573375c068065a638940cab676c652fde367f17eb
  pwcdecode.exe  sha256 d18958228441ecfd928b5bc664008f71a737819be61e7b5ecd4e059489c537a7

The name prefix gives the coded depth (d1/d4/d8) and the picture: shapes, grad, gray (a 256-step grey
ramp coded with -gray), flat, or a size such as 1x5.
