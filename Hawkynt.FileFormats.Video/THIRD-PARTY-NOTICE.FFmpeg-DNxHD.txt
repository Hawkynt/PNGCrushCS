FFmpeg DNxHD reference notice
============================

The DNxHD encoder implementation was developed from SMPTE ST 2019-1 and cross-checked against the
FFmpeg VC-3/DNxHD encoder and decoder, including header construction, coding-unit sizing, entropy
coding order and interlaced macroblock interpretation.

FFmpeg source consulted:
  libavcodec/dnxhdenc.c
  libavcodec/dnxhddec.c
  libavcodec/dnxhddata.c
  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
