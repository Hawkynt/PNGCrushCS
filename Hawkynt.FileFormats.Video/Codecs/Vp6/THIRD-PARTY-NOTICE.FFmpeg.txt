The On2 VP6 decoder in this directory is converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/vp6.c              frame header, vector and coefficient models, both coefficient
                                codings, the bicubic and bilinear prediction filters
  libavcodec/vp6data.h          VP6's own tables
  libavcodec/vp56.c             macroblock types and statistics, motion-vector prediction, the
                                direct-current predictor, motion compensation, the reconstruction
                                loop and the VP6A alpha bitstream
  libavcodec/vp56data.c         the tables VP6 shares with VP5 (taken from ../Vp5)
  libavcodec/vp6dsp.c           the two-dimensional four-tap filter
  libavcodec/vp3dsp.c           the inverse DCT in its full, ten-coefficient and DC-only forms, and
                                the loop filter with its bounding values
  libavcodec/h264chroma_template.c  the eighth-sample bilinear filter
  libavcodec/huffman.c          the Huffman tree construction VP6's Huffman mode uses

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The tables in Vp6Tables.cs were
transcribed mechanically, by a script that parsed vp6data.h's C initialisers, and are reproduced
exactly because they are fixed codec data required for interoperability.

The encoder (Vp6Encoder.cs, ../Vp6VideoEncoder.cs) is an original inverse of this decoder. FFmpeg has
no VP6 encoder, so nothing of an encoder is translated; FFmpeg's decoder is the oracle that reads what
it writes.
