The Sierra VMD video decoder helpers in this directory and
Codecs/VmdVideoDecoder.cs are converted from FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org/):

  libavcodec/vmdvideo.c    Sierra VMD video decoder and its LZSS/RLE coding
  libavformat/sierravmd.c  Sierra VMD block/frame table interpretation

The algorithms were converted into the repository's own C# structure and
conventions rather than pasted as C. The marker-present and markerless LZSS
initialisations, method-3 pair RLE, previous-picture copy semantics and fixed
block/part table layout are format behaviour carried across from those files.
The 770-byte palette-update layout and its first-index/count semantics were
cross-checked against MultimediaWiki's VMD description and ScummVM's
video/coktel_decoder.cpp; no ScummVM implementation code is copied here.
