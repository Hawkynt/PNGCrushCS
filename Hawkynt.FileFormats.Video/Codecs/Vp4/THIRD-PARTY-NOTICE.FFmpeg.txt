The VP4 decoder in this directory, and its tables, are converted from FFmpeg
(https://ffmpeg.org), whose VP3 decoder is where VP4 was reverse-engineered.
On2 published no specification for it.

Files used, all from libavcodec:

  vp3.c        the VP4 paths: the key frame header, vp4_unpack_macroblocks and
               vp4_get_mb_count, unpack_modes, unpack_vectors and vp4_get_mv,
               vp4_unpack_dct_coeffs, vp4_unpack_vlcs and the VP4 DC predictor,
               init_dequantizer, render_slice and vp4_mc_loop_filter
  vp3dsp.c     the inverse transform, the DC-only add, the loop filters and
               their bounding table, and the no-rounding diagonal average
  vp4data.h    every VP4 table: the dequantisation matrix and scale factors,
               the filter limits, the block-pattern and motion-vector codes and
               the eighty coefficient tables
  vp3data.h    the mode code lengths, the end-of-block and zero-run tables and
               the coefficient values of each token
