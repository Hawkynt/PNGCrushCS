The Indeo Video Interactive 4 and 5 decoders in this directory are converted from
FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org/), in particular libavcodec/ivi.c, libavcodec/ivi.h,
libavcodec/ivi_dsp.c, libavcodec/ivi_dsp.h, libavcodec/indeo4.c,
libavcodec/indeo4data.h, libavcodec/indeo5.c and libavcodec/indeo5data.h, together
with the ff_zigzag_direct table from libavcodec/mathtables.c.
