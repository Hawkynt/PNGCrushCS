The Intel Indeo 2 and Intel Indeo 3 decoders in this directory, and the decoders
that use them (Codecs/Indeo2VideoDecoder.cs and Codecs/Indeo3VideoDecoder.cs),
are converted from FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org/):

  libavcodec/indeo2.c      Intel Indeo 2 decoder
  libavcodec/indeo2data.h  its Huffman table and its four delta tables
  libavcodec/indeo3.c      Intel Indeo 3 decoder
  libavcodec/indeo3data.h  its twenty-four vector-quantisation tables

  Copyright (c) 2009 - 2011 Maxim Poliakovski     (indeo3.c, indeo3data.h)

The Indeo Video Interactive 4 and Indeo Video Interactive 5 decoders in this
directory, and the decoders that use them (Codecs/Indeo4VideoDecoder.cs and
Codecs/Indeo5VideoDecoder.cs), are converted from FFmpeg as well:

  libavcodec/ivi.c         the layer both formats share: planes, bands, tiles,
  libavcodec/ivi.h         macroblocks, blocks, motion compensation, the
                           codebooks and the run-value maps
  libavcodec/ivi_dsp.c     the slant and Haar transforms, the wavelet
  libavcodec/ivi_dsp.h     recompositions and the block filters
  libavcodec/indeo4.c      the Indeo 4 picture header
  libavcodec/indeo4data.h  its scans, its eighteen quantisation matrices and
                           the index map that names them
  libavcodec/indeo5.c      the Indeo 5 picture header
  libavcodec/indeo5data.h  its ten base quantisation matrices and eight scale
                           tables
  libavcodec/mathtables.c  the ff_zigzag_direct scan

The algorithm was carried across rather than the source pasted: the code here is
written to this repository's own conventions and its structure follows what the
formats do rather than the layout of the C. What was copied exactly, because it
has to be, is every constant. None of the four codecs transmits its tables —
Indeo 2 names one of four delta tables in two bits of a frame header, Indeo 3
names one of twenty-four quantisation sets in four bits of a cell descriptor, and
Indeo 4 and 5 name a codebook, a scan and a quantisation matrix by index in a
band header — so the numbers themselves are part of the codec, and a decoder
holding different ones decodes a different picture. They are reproduced from the
data headers above without alteration, including the entries a formula in
indeo3.c computes and then overwrites by hand for compatibility with Intel's own
decoders.
