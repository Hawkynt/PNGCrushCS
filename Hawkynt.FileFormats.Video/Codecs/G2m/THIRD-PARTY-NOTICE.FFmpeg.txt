The Go2Meeting decoder in this directory (with G2mVideoDecoder.cs beside it)
is converted from FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), whose decoder is where the
format was reverse-engineered. Its maker published no specification for it.

Files used, all from libavcodec:

  g2meet.c     the chunk layer, the display information, the ePIC tile coder
               and its pixel cache, the JPEG part and its quantisation
               matrices, the Kempf tiles, and the cursor's shape and blending
  elsdec.c     the ELS binary arithmetic decoder, its ladder of rungs, its
               allowable-value table and its exp-Golomb unsigned numbers
