HapBlockEncoding.cs is a conversion of libavcodec/texturedspenc.c from FFmpeg
(https://ffmpeg.org): the DXT1, DXT5 and Scaled YCoCg DXT5 block compression the
Hap encoder in this package writes its textures with — the principal-axis endpoint
fit and its power iteration, the one-dimensional index match, the least-squares
endpoint refinement, the DXT5 alpha index derivation, the RGB-to-YCoCg transform,
and the expand5, expand6, match5 and match6 tables copied value for value.

The frame layout HapVideoEncoder.cs writes — the eight-byte top-level section
header, the pixel format in the type byte's low nibble and the second-stage
compressor in its high one, and the rule that a Snappy result no smaller than the
texture is dropped in favour of the texture — is the Hap specification's, published
by Vidvox at documentation/HapVideoDRAFT.md, and was read there rather than out of
libavcodec/hapenc.c, which is LGPL-2.1-or-later.

HapSnappyEncoder.cs is not taken from anywhere. Snappy's block format is a
decoder's contract and states nothing about which elements a compressor chooses;
the choices there are this package's own, and the format they are written in comes
from Google's own format_description.txt.
