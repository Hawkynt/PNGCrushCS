The Duck/On2 TrueMotion 1 and TrueMotion 2 decoders in this directory, and the
public codec adapters beside them, are converted from FFmpeg
(https://ffmpeg.org/):

  libavcodec/truemotion1.c      TrueMotion 1 frame/header and predictor decoder
  libavcodec/truemotion1data.h  TrueMotion 1 delta and predictor-vector tables
  libavcodec/truemotion2.c      TrueMotion 2 streams, Huffman coding and blocks
  libavcodec/vlc.c              code assignment used by TrueMotion 2 trees

  Copyright (c) 2005 Konstantin Shishkov                (truemotion2.c)

The algorithms are carried across into managed C# rather than vendored. The
TrueMotion 1 numeric tables are reproduced exactly because they are fixed codec
data required for interoperability. FFmpeg's truemotion1data.h records that
those data originally appeared in On2's VpVision and explains FFmpeg's treatment
of necessary decoding tables as non-expressive data; PNGCrushCS follows the same
rule stated in AGENTS.md for codec tables and constants.

The encoders are original inverse implementations. FFmpeg supplies decoders for
TrueMotion 1 and TrueMotion 2 but no encoder for either. The write paths therefore
use the decoded bitstream grammar and fixed tables as interoperability facts and
produce conservative subsets of the formats rather than translating an external
encoder.
