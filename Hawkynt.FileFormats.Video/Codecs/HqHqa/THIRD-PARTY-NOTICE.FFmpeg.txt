The Canopus HQ and HQA decoder in this directory is converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/hq_hqa.c      the HQ and HQA frame and slice layouts, the macroblocks and blocks
  libavcodec/hq_hqadsp.c   the inverse transform
  libavcodec/hq_hqadata.h  the quantiser matrices and map, the AC code, the profiles and their macroblock orders
  libavcodec/hq_common.c   the coded-block-pattern code, shared with HQX

this repository.

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The tables in HqHqaTables.cs were
transcribed mechanically, by a script that parsed the C initialisers, and are reproduced exactly because
they are fixed data required for interoperability. The encoder is this package's own.
