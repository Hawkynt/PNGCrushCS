The Sorenson Video 3 decoder in this directory (with Svq3VideoDecoder.cs beside
it) is converted from FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), whose decoder is where the
format was reverse-engineered. Its maker published no specification for it.

Files used, all from libavcodec:

  svq3.c           the sequence header, the watermark, the slice header, the
                   macroblock layer, motion prediction and compensation, the
                   coefficient coding and its run/level tables, the transform
                   and its dequantisation table, and the picture management
  h264pred.c,      the intra 4x4, 16x16 and chroma predictors, with SVQ3's own
  h264pred_template.c  down-left and plane variants
  h264_parse.c     the intra mode checks against neighbour availability
  h264data.c,      the coded-block-pattern, intra macroblock and chroma QP
  h264data.h       tables
  h264idct_template.c  the chroma DC transform
  hpeldsp.c, hpel_template.c, tpeldsp.c   half- and third-sample motion
                   compensation
