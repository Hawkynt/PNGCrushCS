The YLC decoder in this directory is converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/ylc.c   the packet layout, the count tables and build_vlc/get_tree_codes' Huffman
                     construction, the group coding with its residue tables and runs, and the
                     gradient prediction

  The file carries no copyright line of its own; it is the FFmpeg developers' work.

option) any later version. This work is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License
version 3 or later, which the "or later" clause permits; the licence text is in LICENSE at the root of
this repository.

The algorithm is carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The four residue tables in YlcTables.cs
were transcribed mechanically, by a script that parsed the C initialisers, and are reproduced exactly
because they are fixed codec data required for interoperability. The encoder is this package's own.
