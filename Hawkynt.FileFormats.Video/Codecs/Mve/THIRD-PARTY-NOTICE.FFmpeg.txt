The Interplay MVE true-colour and legacy video decoding rules in this directory are based on the public Interplay MVE/Video bitstream descriptions and on FFmpeg's Interplay decoder as a behavioral/reference implementation for rules the public description does not specify completely.

Reference implementation:

  FFmpeg libavcodec/interplayvideo.c
  Copyright (C) 2003 The FFmpeg project

FFmpeg distributes that decoder under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or (at your option) any later version. This repository is distributed under LGPL-3.0-or-later, which is compatible with that "or later" grant.

The implementation here is written to this repository's C# architecture rather than mechanically copied from the C source. Required bitstream behavior was carried across: block-mode meanings, motion-vector mappings, the separate true-colour motion stream, RGB555 control-bit conventions, and the 0x06/0x10 page-update behavior. The public format description and FFmpeg are also used as behavioral oracles for tests.
