The Chronomaster DFA decoder in this directory, and the DFA container in Formats/Dfa, are converted
from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/dfa.c      the chunk decoders, the 6-bit palette, and the interleaved version 0x100 layout
  libavformat/dfa.c     the DFIA header and the frames each EOFR chunk ends
  libavutil/mem.c       av_memcpy_backptr's handling of short and zero distances

  and the FFmpeg developers named in the files above.

option) any later version. This work is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License
version 3 or later, which the "or later" clause permits; the licence text is in LICENSE at the root of
this repository.

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. One comparison in the DDS1 skip is
deliberately turned round from FFmpeg's, which refuses every DDS1 chunk; DfaCodec.cs says why. The
encoder and the container writer are this package's own.
