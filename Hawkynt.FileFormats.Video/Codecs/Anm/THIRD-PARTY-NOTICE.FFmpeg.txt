The Deluxe Paint Animation decoder in this directory, and the Deluxe Paint Animation container in
Formats/DeluxePaintAnm, are converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/anm.c      the RunSkipDump operations and the header's palette
  libavformat/anm.c     the large-page header, the page table, and the order records are read in

are this package's own.
