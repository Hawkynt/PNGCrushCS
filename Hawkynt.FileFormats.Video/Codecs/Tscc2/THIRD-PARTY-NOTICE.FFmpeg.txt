The TechSmith Screen Codec 2 decoder in this directory is converted from FFmpeg
(https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/tscc2.c      the frame layout, the macroblock modes and the inverse transform
  libavcodec/tscc2data.h  the quantisers and the DC, count and AC code tables
  libavcodec/mathtables.c ff_zigzag_scan

this repository.

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The tables in Tscc2Tables.cs were
transcribed mechanically, by a script that parsed the C initialisers, and are reproduced exactly because
they are fixed data required for interoperability. The encoder is this package's own.
