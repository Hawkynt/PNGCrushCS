The Apple Video (RPZA) bitstream written by AppleVideoEncoder in this package is adapted from
FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg), in particular libavcodec/rpzaenc.c: the order the three
codings are tried in, the skip test and its threshold, the running minimum, maximum, total and
average that decide whether a run of blocks is one colour and how far a further block may stray from
it, the two thresholds those tests use, the rule that a multi-block opcode stops at thirty-two
blocks and at the end of a block row, the discipline of updating the previous-frame buffer for every
block that was coded and no block that was skipped, and the chunk header.

The four-colour coding is not taken from there and is not written at all. The reference's own
default thresholds never select it, and forcing it shows why that went unnoticed: it indexes its
colour triple blue-first, as the bitstream numbers channels, and packs it red-first, so the block
carries red and blue exchanged; and it clips the endpoints of its least-squares fit to eight bits
before shifting them into a five-bit field. AppleVideoEncoder writes such a block as sixteen
colours, which is exact.

This notice names the codec in its file name because the directory it sits in holds more than one
codec's source.
