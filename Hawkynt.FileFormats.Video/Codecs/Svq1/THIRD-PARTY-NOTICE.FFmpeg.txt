The Sorenson Video 1 decoder in this directory, and the vector-quantisation search in its encoder, are
converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/svq1dec.c   the frame header and its scrambling, the quadtree walk, multi-stage vector
                         reconstruction in packed four-sample words, half-sample motion with its median
                         prediction, and the four-vector mode
  libavcodec/svq1enc.c   encode_block: the codebook-stage and split search the encoder uses (its
                         motion estimation, which rests on FFmpeg's MPEG encoder, is not taken)
  libavcodec/svq1_cb.h   the intra and inter codebooks
  libavcodec/svq1_vlc.h  the block-type, stage-count and mean codes
  libavcodec/svq1.c      the frame-size table
  libavcodec/h263data.c  the motion-vector codes SVQ1 shares with H.263

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The tables in Svq1Tables.cs were
transcribed mechanically, by a script that parsed the C initialisers, and are reproduced exactly because
they are fixed codec data required for interoperability. The encoder's motion search, mode decision,
bit assembly and packetisation are this package's own.
