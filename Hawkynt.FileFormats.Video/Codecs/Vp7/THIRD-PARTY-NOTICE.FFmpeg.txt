The On2 VP7 decoder in this directory is converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/vp8.c      the VP7 paths: vp7_decode_frame_header and its fading, vp7_get_quants, the
                        twelve-neighbour census of vp7_decode_mvs with vp7_calculate_mb_offset, VP7's
                        motion vector components, inter_predict_dc, decode_block_coeffs with its VP7
                        end-of-block rule, the carried left contexts, and VP7's loop filter limits
                        and edge order in filter_mb and filter_level_for_mb
  libavcodec/vp8dsp.c   vp7_idct_add_c, vp7_idct_dc_add_c, vp7_luma_dc_wht_c, vp7_luma_dc_wht_dc_c and
                        the vp7 loop filters
  libavcodec/vp8data.h  the VP7 quantiser tables, census predictors and weights, mode contexts,
                        default motion vector probabilities and feature value sizes

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The tables in Vp7Tables.cs were
transcribed mechanically, by a script that parsed the C initialisers, and are reproduced exactly because
they are fixed codec data required for interoperability. What VP7 shares with VP8 comes from this
package's own VP8 decoder. The encoder in Vp7Encoder.cs is this package's own; FFmpeg has no VP7 encoder.
