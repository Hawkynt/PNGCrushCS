The ScreenPressor decoder in this directory is converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/scpr.c   the frame types, both range coders, the adaptive models and key and inter frames
  libavcodec/scpr.h   the run operations
  libavcodec/scpr3.c  the third generation: its rANS coder, its adaptive tables and colour models, and its
                      key and inter frames
  libavcodec/scpr3.h  the third generation's model layouts
