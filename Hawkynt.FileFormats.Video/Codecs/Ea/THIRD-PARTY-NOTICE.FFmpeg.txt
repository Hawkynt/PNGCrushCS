The Electronic Arts TQI, TGQ, Madcow (MAD) and TGV decoders in this directory, and the parts of the EA
container reader in Formats/Ea that find their chunks, are converted from FFmpeg
(https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/eatqi.c          TQI: the quality-derived matrix and the MPEG-1 intra macroblocks
  libavcodec/eatgq.c          TGQ: its matrix, its own coefficient code, and its macroblock modes
  libavcodec/eamad.c          MAD: key and predicted pictures, motion compensation, the matrix
  libavcodec/eatgv.c          TGV: the LZ77 key frames and the vector-quantised predicted frames
  libavcodec/eaidct.c         the inverse DCT all three DCT codecs share
  libavformat/electronicarts.c the chunks each codec arrives in, and their byte order
  libavcodec/aandcttab.c      ff_inv_aanscales
  libavcodec/mpeg12data.c     ff_mpeg1_default_intra_matrix
  libavcodec/mathtables.c     ff_zigzag_direct

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The tables in EaVideo.cs are
reproduced exactly because they are fixed data required for interoperability. The four encoders in
EaEncoders.cs and the additions to the container writer are this package's own; FFmpeg has no encoder
for any of these codecs.
