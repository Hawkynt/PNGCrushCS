The NewTek SpeedHQ decoder in this directory, and the DC and AC block coding in its encoder, are
converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/speedhqdec.c  fields and slices, DC prediction, the AC run-level loop with its escape,
                           run-length and DCT alpha, and the half-macroblock border column
  libavcodec/speedhqenc.c  encode_dc and encode_block: the DC and AC bit coding the encoder writes
                           (its picture layer, which rests on FFmpeg's MPEG encoder, is not taken)
  libavcodec/speedhq.c     the AC codes, levels and runs
  libavcodec/mpeg12data.c  the MPEG-2 DC size codes SpeedHQ shares

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The tables in SpeedHqTables.cs are
reproduced exactly because they are fixed codec data required for interoperability. The encoder's
forward transform, quantisation, slice assembly, border column and alpha coding are this package's own.
