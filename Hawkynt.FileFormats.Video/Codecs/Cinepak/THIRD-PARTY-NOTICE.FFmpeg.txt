The Cinepak bitstream written by CinepakVideoEncoder in this package is adapted from FFmpeg
(https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg), in particular libavcodec/cinepakenc.c: the chunk types and
their order, the strip header, the interleaving of flag words with vector references, the
per-block bit costs of the three vector codings, and the fixed-point RGB-to-YUV coefficients.

FFmpeg is distributed under the LGPL-2.1-or-later, which this package's LGPL-3.0-or-later can
absorb. The file itself additionally carries the permissive grant reproduced below.

The vector quantiser is not taken from there. FFmpeg's uses ELBG seeded from a pseudo-random
generator; this package uses a deterministic farthest-point and Lloyd quantiser of its own.
