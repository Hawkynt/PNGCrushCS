The MS Screen 2 decoder in this directory (with Mss2VideoDecoder.cs beside it)
is converted from FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), whose decoder is where the
format was reverse-engineered. Microsoft published no specification for it.

Files used, all from libavcodec:

  mss2.c       the frame header, the WMV9 rectangle list, the second arithmetic
               coder (arith2), the palette update, the run-length and 15-bit
               modes, the draw region and slice split, and the WMV9 picture
               header variant and rectangle decode
  mss12.c      the adaptive models, the pixel contexts and colour cache, and
               the region coder: splits, flat, coded, copied, moved and masked
               regions
  mss2dsp.c    the WMV9 rectangle's YUV to RGB blit, its masked form, the grey
               fill and the half-resolution upsampling
  vc1.c        ff_vc1_parse_frame_header's MSS2 branch
  vc1_loopfilter.c, vc1dsp.c
               the I-picture loop filter, now in ../Vc1/Vc1LoopFilter.cs
