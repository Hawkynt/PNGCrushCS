The 8088flex TMV decoder in this directory, and the TMV container in Formats/Tmv, are converted from
FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/tmv.c          the screen layout and the drawing of each character cell
  libavformat/tmv.c         the TMV header, its features, and the interleaving of screens and sound
  libavutil/xga_font_data.c the IBM CGA 8x8 font
  libavcodec/cga_data.c     the sixteen-colour CGA palette

  and the FFmpeg developers named in the files above.

option) any later version. This work is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License
version 3 or later, which the "or later" clause permits; the licence text is in LICENSE at the root of
this repository.

The algorithms are carried across into managed C#, not vendored. The font and palette in TmvTables.cs
were transcribed mechanically, by a script that parsed the C initialisers, and are reproduced exactly
because they are fixed data required for interoperability. The encoder and the container writer are
this package's own.
