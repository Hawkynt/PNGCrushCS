The SheerVideo decoder in this directory is converted from FFmpeg (https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg):

  libavcodec/sheervideo.c       the layouts, the code tables' construction and the 25 row decoders
  libavcodec/sheervideodata.h   the code-length tables

option) any later version. This work is distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License
version 3 or later, which the "or later" clause permits; the licence text is in LICENSE at the root of
this repository.

SheerVideoDecode.cs was produced mechanically from those sources by a script that turns each pointer into
an offset into a plane and masks each store to its sample width; its tables are reproduced exactly because
they are fixed data required for interoperability. The encoder is this package's own.
