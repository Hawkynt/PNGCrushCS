The four pairs of quantisation weight matrices in ProResProfile.cs — one pair
for each of the 4:2:2 profiles 422 Proxy, 422 LT, 422 and 422 HQ — are the
tables FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org) carries as constants in
libavcodec/proresenc_kostya.c, under the name prores_quant_matrices.

SMPTE RDD 36 prints none of them: it describes a decoding process, and a
decoder is told the matrices by every frame it reads, so the only place a
profile's own matrices exist in writing is in an encoder. Each of the four
pairs here was additionally read back byte for byte out of the frame headers of
files written by both of FFmpeg's ProRes encoders, so the copies are confirmed
against the bitstream as well as against the source.

Nothing else in this directory comes from FFmpeg. The bitstream syntax, the
entropy codes, the block scans, the transforms and the rate decision are
written from SMPTE RDD 36:2022 and are cited by clause in the source.

  proresenc_kostya.c   prores_quant_matrices — the four 4:2:2 profiles' luma
                       and chroma quantisation weight matrices
